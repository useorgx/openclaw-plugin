Workstream: Hook Research & Content Strategy  
Scope: Practical benchmark synthesis to guide OrgX LinkedIn carousel hooks, structure, and CTA strategy for the next 30 days.

## 1) Current benchmark signals (what to trust)

| Source | Dataset / period | Key finding | Why it matters for this sprint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Reliability notes

- Both sources are vendor studies using proprietary methodology and platform-level samples (useful directional input, not universal truth).
- Socialinsider reports rates by impressions, while Metricool uses a different engagement-rate framing; compare trend direction, not absolute percentages.
- This audit is a strategy artifact, not a raw post-level scrape.

## 2) B2B SaaS/dev tools implications

1. Default to carousel/native document for educational thought leadership and workflow teardowns.
2. Use hook slide first; no logo-first or product-UI-first openings.
3. Keep each deck to one operational pain and one measurable promised outcome.
4. Place proof early (slides 2-4): numeric benchmark, before/after workflow, or short case evidence.
5. Use one CTA type per post (comment keyword, DM prompt, or link-in-comment) to avoid mixed-intent drop-off.

## 3) Hook patterns to test (Larry playbook aligned)

1. Contrarian: "Most teams automate the wrong step first."
2. Cost of inaction: "Your dispatch handoff is burning 5+ engineer-hours/week."
3. Teardown: "We rebuilt this incident-response loop in 7 slides."
4. Artifact-led: "Steal this on-call triage checklist."
5. Decision framing: "Pick A for speed, B for control."

## 4) Carousel format guardrails

- Slide 1: sharp claim, <=14 words, no jargon.
- Slide 2: quantify tension (time loss, risk, or missed pipeline).
- Slides 3-6: workflow sequence + evidence + objection handling.
- Final slide: one CTA and one next action.
- Slide count: 6-9.
- Mobile readability required (standalone comprehension per slide).

## 5) 30-day test plan

- Cadence: 2 carousels/week x 4 weeks (8 total posts).
- Keep posting windows stable to reduce timing noise.

### Experiment matrix

| Variable | Test arms | KPI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook family | Contrarian vs Teardown vs Artifact-led | Saves/post, comments/post |
| Proof style | Numeric benchmark vs mini-case snippet | Last-slide impressions / first-slide impressions |
| CTA mode | Comment keyword vs DM prompt vs link-in-comment | Qualified inbound conversations |

## 6) Execution decisions for next sprint

1. Set LinkedIn pillar format to carousel for the upcoming educational sequence.
2. Build first 8 posts around teardown + artifact-led hooks.
3. Track four metrics per post: saves, comments, inbound DMs, attributed meeting/demo starts.
